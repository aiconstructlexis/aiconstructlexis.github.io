# LiRA
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 2  

## State of the Field

Likelihood Ratio Attack, a membership inference attack that formulates the problem as hypothesis testing, comparing the likelihood of a model's output score under the hypotheses that the sample was or was not in the training data.
