# Wikipedia
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 5  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited work, Wikipedia is most commonly treated as an evaluation resource, described either as an external knowledge base or as a text corpus. Definitions vary by use: one paper uses it as the source of retrieved documents for RAG evaluation, drawing on a Wikimedia dump provided by Cohere, while others refer to it as a pre-training dataset or as MassiveText Wikipedia used to test whether findings extend to a more complex corpus and larger scales.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Across these papers, Wikipedia is not linked to any construct or behavioral indicator, so no explicit claim about what it measures is recorded.

**Space of uses.** Papers use Wikipedia in several evaluation roles: as the retrieval source in RAG experiments, as a corpus considered when computing Diff-eRank and reduced loss, and as a supporting evaluation setting for robustness checks. No other measurement instruments are linked to it in these papers.
