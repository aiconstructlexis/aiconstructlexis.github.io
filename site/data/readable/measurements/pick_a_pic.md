# Pick-a-Pic
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 3  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** In the description, Pick-a-Pic is framed as a large-scale open dataset derived from a web platform that collects real users' creative intention, and papers present it as being used to evaluate generation alignment with user preference.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link Pick-a-Pic to one construct, Text-image alignment, and to no behavioral indicators. In the recorded measured_by relationship, it is used to measure Text-image alignment, with one paper describing it as one of several text-to-image evaluation benchmarks used to score agreement with human judgments of alignment for images generated from complex text prompts, so the reported claims converge on a single construct rather than spreading across many.

**Space of uses.** The recorded relationships do not link Pick-a-Pic to other measurement instruments, but one paper positions it alongside DrawBench, EditBench, COCO-T2I, and TIFA160 as one of five text-to-image evaluation benchmarks. In that usage, it appears as part of a benchmark set for evaluating alignment against human judgments on generated images.
