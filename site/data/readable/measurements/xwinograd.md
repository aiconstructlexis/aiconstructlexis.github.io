# XWinograd
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 4  
**Also known as:** Xwinograd  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed work, it is described as a multilingual or cross-lingual benchmark or task based on the Winograd Schema Challenge for pronoun resolution. Definitions differ slightly in emphasis: one presents it as a benchmark for commonsense reasoning that tests pronoun resolution, while another presents it as a cross-lingual pronoun resolution task testing coreference and syntactic understanding in diverse languages.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Recorded relationships link it to 1 behavioral indicator, Coreference resolution, and to no constructs. Papers use it to measure coreference resolution, including describing it as "a cross-lingual adaptation of the Winograd Schema challenge ... for coreference resolution evaluation," so the reported measurement claims converge on that single behavioral indicator rather than spreading across multiple targets.

**Space of uses.** Papers position it as an evaluation task, not as something linked to other measurement instruments. One quoted passage places it among "Discriminative Tasks," another glosses it as "coreference resolution (Xwinograd)," and no measurement instrument is linked to it.
