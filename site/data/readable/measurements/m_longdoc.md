# M-LongDoc
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 1  

## State of the Field

A benchmark of 851 samples designed to evaluate multimodal long-document understanding through open-ended question answering over real-world documents spanning hundreds of pages, with questions requiring in-depth reasoning across text, figures, and tables.
