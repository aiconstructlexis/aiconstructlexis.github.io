# AMC 2023
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 30  
**Also known as:** AMC, AMC2023, AMC23  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across papers, the prevailing description is of a benchmark or dataset drawn from the 2023 American Mathematics Competitions, used for standardized or competition-style math questions. Most definitions frame the content as challenging high school-level problems and describe it as a benchmark, dataset, or exam for mathematical reasoning or problem solving. A smaller line of description differs, calling AMC2023 a middle school math competition covering arithmetic, algebra, and geometry and noting that it contains 40 questions. Papers refer to it under several surface forms, including AMC, AMC2023, AMC23, and AMC 2023.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The measurement claims converge rather than spreading across many targets. Papers link AMC 2023 to one construct, Mathematical reasoning, and one behavioral indicator, Mathematical problem solving, with Mathematical reasoning the prevailing usage and Mathematical problem solving appearing in a smaller line of work. In these uses, papers place it within benchmark sets for assessing models' reasoning or problem-solving abilities on math tasks.

**Space of uses.** Papers usually deploy AMC 2023 as one item in broader math benchmark suites rather than as a standalone instrument. It is commonly used alongside AIME 2024, MATH or MATH500, GSM8K, and OlympiadBench, and some evaluations also pair it with Minerva Math and other competition benchmarks. Authors often position it as a challenging, out-of-distribution, competition-level, or olympiad-level benchmark, and some report pass@1 or pass@16 on it.
