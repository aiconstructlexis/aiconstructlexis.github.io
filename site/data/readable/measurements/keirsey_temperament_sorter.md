# Keirsey Temperament Sorter
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 1  

## State of the Field

A self-assessment personality questionnaire, based on the Myers-Briggs Type Indicator, used to classify individuals into one of four temperament groups: Rationals, Idealists, Guardians, and Artisans.
