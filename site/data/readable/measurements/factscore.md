# FActScore
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 35  
**Also known as:** FactScore, FActscore, FACTSCORE, FACTSCORES, FactScore-Bio  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Most commonly described as a reference-based evaluation procedure for long-form generation, especially biographies, papers say it decomposes generated text into atomic facts or claims and verifies each one against a knowledge source, reference passages, or retrieved Wikipedia evidence. Across definitions, authors alternately call it a tool, benchmark, framework, suite, fact evaluator, or automated retrieval augmentation pipeline, and describe outputs as a precision or percentage of supported atomic facts, or counts of true and false facts. A smaller naming family in the papers includes FACTSCORES, defined as the proportion of cited sub-facts that are supported, and FactScore-Bio, described as a long-form QA dataset of biography questions from Wikipedia.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link it to 2 constructs and 3 behavioral indicators. The prevailing usage is to measure Factuality: papers describe it as reference-based truthfulness scoring, as evaluating factuality of biographies or generated summaries, and as assessing factuality degradation by comparing generations with original text. A smaller line of work uses it to measure Faithfulness, including faithful biography generation and factual consistency of condensations. Among behavioral indicators, papers also use it to measure Biography generation and Hallucination, while Claim decomposition is only described as related to it.

**Space of uses.** Papers typically use it as an evaluation benchmark or automatic check over model outputs, following its decomposition-then-verify procedure to extract correct and incorrect facts or to run a decomposition engine and verifier. Some position it alongside TruthfulQA as a benchmark, discuss it alongside SAFE, compare it with FAITHSCORE, or derive claim datasets and decomposition policies from it. One paper also relates it to the instrument Biography, describing FactScore as the corresponding evaluation strategy for that test setting.
