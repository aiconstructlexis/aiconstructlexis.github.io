# NewsQA
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 6  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, NewsQA is most commonly described as a question answering or machine comprehension dataset built from news articles, with questions answered from long passages. Definitions emphasize comprehension, extraction of answers from realistic passages, and, in one description, synthesizing information from long passages. A narrower characterization specifies CNN articles paired with questions and answers derived from those articles, and one quoted passage refers specifically to the MRQA version of NewsQA.

**What it is claimed to measure.** A paper uses NewsQA to measure Question answering. Across the recorded links, it is connected to one behavioral indicator, Question answering, and to no constructs, so the reported measurement use is concentrated rather than spread across multiple constructs.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it as an evaluation dataset for question answering over news passages or articles. One quoted passage lists the MRQA version of NewsQA alongside HotpotQA among the datasets tested, and another paper uses it for the news articles domain when evaluating takedown methods. No other measurement instrument is linked to it.
