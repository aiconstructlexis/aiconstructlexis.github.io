# OLMES
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 3  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers describe OLMES as an evaluation suite for language models, covering multiple-choice and cloze-format tasks across knowledge and reasoning domains; one definition also says it uses manually curated in-context examples and calibration methods to improve reliability. Its contents are presented as a suite of benchmark tasks, with papers listing MMLU, ARC-Easy, ARC-Challenge, and CommonsenseQA among them, and another description also including BoolQ in a suite of 10 multiple choice question answering benchmarks.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The measurement claims converge on one behavioral indicator rather than spreading across constructs. Papers link OLMES to 1 behavioral indicator, Multiple-choice question answering, and to no constructs, and one paper explicitly describes it as a suite of multiple choice question answering benchmarks.

**Space of uses.** In use, papers adopt OLMES for evaluation and present it as a grouped suite of benchmarks. Across papers it is related to 10 instruments, including ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, OpenBookQA, PIQA, and WinoGrande.
