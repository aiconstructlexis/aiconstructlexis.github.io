# Open LLM Leaderboard V1
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 3  
**Also known as:** Hugging Face Open LLM Leaderboard v1  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the definitions, the prevailing framing is of a public leaderboard and benchmark suite for open-source large language models. One paper describes v1 as evaluating and ranking models with publicly available weights, while another specifies that it covers MMLU, ARC-Challenge, GSM8K, HellaSwag, Winogrande, and TruthfulQA, using both log-likelihood and text-generation evaluations.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers describe it as assessing question-answering and reasoning tasks, and the task-level descriptions attached to the suite mention grade school math, world knowledge and reasoning, language understanding, and factual correctness. No named constructs or behavioral indicators are linked to it in the recorded relationships.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it as a public, and in one description popular, leaderboard that evaluates and ranks models with publicly available weights. Among the recorded instrument links, it is related to GSM8K and MMLU, and one paper states that these benchmarks are included in it; the suite definition also places it alongside ARC-Challenge, HellaSwag, Winogrande, and TruthfulQA.
