# WildChat-1M
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 2  

## State of the Field

Real-world dataset of 837,989 conversations from 167,062 users interacting with a ChatGPT mirror site over one year, used to analyze conceptual diversity trends before and after GPT version updates.
