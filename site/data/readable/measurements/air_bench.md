# AIR-Bench
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 12  
**Also known as:** AirBench, AIR-bench  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed papers, AIR-Bench is most commonly treated as an aggregated or holistic evaluation suite for large audio or audio-language models, covering audio understanding or general auditory processing across speech, audio, and music. Some authors describe it more specifically as an open-ended question-answering benchmark, and another paper refers to AirBench-Chat as a conversational ability evaluation based on text-based interactions. A smaller framing uses the same name for a benchmark dedicated to retrieval performance, with test data automatically generated by large language models without human intervention.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The prevailing usage is to evaluate audio-centered capabilities: papers describe AIR-Bench as assessing audio understanding, general auditory processing, spoken language comprehension, and conversational ability in the AirBench-Chat subset. A smaller line of work presents it as measuring retrieval performance instead. No constructs or behavioral indicators are linked to it by the papers.

**Space of uses.** Papers commonly discuss AIR-Bench alongside AudioBench, often as another aggregated evaluation set for large audio models or as a benchmark that extends to open-ended QA formats. It also appears alongside Audio-Flamingo in comparisons of audio understanding benchmarks, and alongside MTEB in a retrieval-evaluation setting. No measurement instrument is linked to it by the papers.

**Concerns raised.** The concerns that appear are specific rather than general. For AirBench-Chat, papers say the dialogue content is limited to the textual modality and that its conversational ability evaluation is based on text-based interactions rather than spoken dialogue capabilities. Another paper notes that, although AIR-bench expands beyond classification, the number of tasks available for evaluating universal models in its foundation track remains limited.
