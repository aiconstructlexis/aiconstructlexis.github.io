**What it is.** In the usage in the papers, LawBench is treated as a benchmark focused on Chinese laws, with 20 diverse legal tasks. The definition and source text specifically mention charge prediction and prison term prediction, and the paper places LawBench alongside LegalBench when evaluating LLMs' generalization capability across legal domains.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The paper links LawBench to 2 behavioral indicators, charge prediction and prison term prediction, both through measured_by relationships. Its quoted use narrows LawBench to these two tasks, and no constructs are linked to it.

**Space of uses.** The paper uses LawBench as part of a broader legal-domain evaluation and positions it alongside LegalBench. Within LawBench, it concentrates on the two challenging tasks of charge prediction and prison term prediction. No measurement instrument is linked to it.
