# SBIC
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 2  

## State of the Field

The Social Bias Inference Corpus (SBIC) is a labeled dataset of social media posts used for assessing societal biases in language, which served as a source for the CoBia dataset.
