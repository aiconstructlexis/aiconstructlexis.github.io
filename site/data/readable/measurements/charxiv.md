## State of the Field

**What it is.** Papers most commonly describe CharXiv as a benchmark or comprehensive evaluation suite for chart understanding, built from real-world charts drawn from arXiv papers or preprints. Definitions emphasize natural, challenging, and diverse charts, and one description specifies 2,323 charts with hand-curated descriptive and reasoning questions. A smaller framing presents it as a benchmark dataset for evaluating complex visual reasoning through the interpretation of charts and graphs across scientific and technical domains.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The linked measurement claims center on 2 behavioral indicators, Chart and diagram understanding and Complex reasoning question answering, and no construct is linked to CharXiv. One paper says it provides "a more realistic and faithful measure of progress" in MLLM chart understanding. The same paper also presents it as measuring complex reasoning question answering through reasoning questions that require synthesizing information across complex visual elements.

**Space of uses.** No other measurement instrument is linked to CharXiv. Papers present it as an evaluation benchmark: one study reports results on its validation set to compare systems across various domains, particularly on tasks involving complex visual reasoning and interpretation of charts and graphs, and a survey cites it as expanding chart understanding scenarios by incorporating real-world chart data from arXiv preprints.
