# MS-COCO
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 88  
**Also known as:** MSCOCO, MSCOCO-2014, MS COCO, COCO, COCO-2017 validation set, COCO-Val  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed work, the prevailing description is a large-scale vision dataset and benchmark for object detection, segmentation, and captioning, often narrowed to an image-caption dataset with complex scenes and multiple objects, or to the validation set used for zero-shot captioning and text-to-image evaluation. Papers also refer to specific versions and subsets, including MSCOCO-2014, the COCO-2017 validation set, COCO-Val, the Karpathy test split, and an MS-COCO-30K evaluation benchmark. A few descriptions add dataset details, such as images annotated with 80 categories, or a Karpathy-style setup with about 120,000 training images, 5,000 validation and 5,000 test images, and five captions per image.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Rather than converging on a single target, papers link it to 9 behavioral indicators and 4 constructs. The prevailing use is to measure image captioning, with recurring use for cross-modal retrieval and image generation; at the construct level, papers also report it as measuring multimodal alignment and visual generation ability. The remaining links cover 6 other behavioral indicators and 2 other constructs.

**Space of uses.** Most papers position it as a standard benchmark, commonly used alongside Flickr30K, and also paired with NoCaps, TextCaps, and ImageNet; CHAIR appears once as a related instrument, with one paper sampling MSCOCO validation images for it. Many studies use the validation split or Karpathy test split for zero-shot captioning or text-to-image generation, while some use it as a source of prompts, scene images, or annotations.

**Concerns raised.** Concerns appear mainly in retrieval settings. One paper says performance on popular cross-modal retrieval benchmarks such as MS-COCO has reached saturation, and another says standard retrieval benchmarks including MSCOCO lack aesthetic evaluation.
