# NumGLUE
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 7  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across papers, NumGLUE is most commonly described as a suite of mathematical or numerical reasoning tasks, though one definition frames it as a benchmark for numerical and quantitative reasoning. The definitions emphasize tasks that require understanding of numerical concepts and language, handling numbers in natural language contexts, and, in one paper’s phrasing, tasks that fundamentally involve arithmetic formula evaluation.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use NumGLUE to measure Mathematical reasoning. It is linked to 1 construct, Mathematical reasoning, and to no behavioral indicators, so the reported measurement use centers on that single construct; papers place it among in-domain datasets for mathematical reasoning and also group NumGLUE1 and NumGLUE2 under math-related tasks.

**Space of uses.** Papers typically use NumGLUE alongside other evaluation datasets, including GSM8K, MATH, Mathmeticas, SVAMP, and MathQA. In these accounts it appears as one part of a broader evaluation setup, for example as an in-domain dataset in a comprehensive assessment or as one of several benchmarks used to evaluate numerical reasoning.
