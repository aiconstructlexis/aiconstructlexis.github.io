# SciEval
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 4  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across papers, the prevailing description of SciEval is a benchmark for scientific-domain evaluation. Definitions variously present it as a multi-level benchmark for scientific research, a benchmark for understanding and reasoning about scientific content, and a comprehensive, multi-disciplinary benchmark that assesses scientific reasoning, problem solving, and subjective question answering.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use SciEval to evaluate scientific reasoning most often, with closely related descriptions emphasizing scientific research reasoning, problem solving, understanding scientific content, and subjective question-answering capabilities. Papers do not explicitly link it to named constructs or behavioral indicators through measurement relationships, so its measurement role is described in these broader capability terms.

**Space of uses.** In use, papers refer to "benchmark tests on SciEval" and place it within broader evaluation suites alongside instruments such as MedQA, MedMCQA, FinEval, FinanceIQ, and MMLU-Sci. They position it as a multi-level scientific benchmark, and one description further calls it a comprehensive, multi-disciplinary benchmark for subjective question answering.
