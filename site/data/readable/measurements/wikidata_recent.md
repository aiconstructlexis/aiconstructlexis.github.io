# WikiData_recent
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 4  
**Also known as:** WikiDatarecent  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** In the papers, WikiData_recent is described as a dataset for knowledge editing evaluation that focuses on recent, updated facts from Wikidata. Papers place it among experimental datasets for knowledge insertion and knowledge modification.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use it to measure one behavioral indicator and two constructs. The linked claims report it as measuring Knowledge editing, and as evaluating Editing locality and Fluency, so its reported use covers a small set of targets rather than only one.

**Space of uses.** Papers use it as one dataset within broader knowledge editing evaluation suites, alongside WikiDatacounterfact, WikiBio, ConvSent, and ZsRE. One paper also places it in experiments that cover both knowledge insertion and knowledge modification.
