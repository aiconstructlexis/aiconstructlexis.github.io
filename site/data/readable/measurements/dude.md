# DUDE
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 6  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the papers, the prevailing framing is as a benchmark for multi-page document understanding, with coverage limited to documents up to 20 pages. One paper gives a more task-specific account, describing DUDE as a multi-page visual question answering dataset containing financial reports, and highlighting the "diverse domains and complex document structures" associated with it.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers do not formally link DUDE to any construct or behavioral indicator. Instead, they describe it at the task level: most commonly as a benchmark for multi-page document understanding, and in one paper as a dataset used to evaluate retrieval and question answering performance.

**Space of uses.** No measurement instrument is formally linked to DUDE, but papers do use it alongside other benchmarks. One paper reports using DUDE together with MPVQA, CUAD, and MOAMOB, while another discusses DUDE alongside MP-DocVQA as an example of a current multi-page document benchmark.

**Concerns raised.** The main limitation raised is document-length coverage. As noted in LongDocURL: a Comprehensive Multimodal Long Document Benchmark Integrating Understanding, Reasoning, and Locating, current multi-page document benchmarks "such as MP-DocVQA and DUDE ... do not assess documents exceeding 20 pages," matching the accompanying description of DUDE as covering documents up to 20 pages.
