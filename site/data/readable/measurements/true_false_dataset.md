# True-False dataset
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 3  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** The paper treats the True-False dataset as a labeled collection of factoid statements marked True or False, covering six disjoint topics: Cities, Inventions, Chemical Elements, Animals, Companies, and Scientific Facts. It is described as a constructed dataset for factuality probes, and no alternative versions, subsets, or competing format descriptions are reported.

**What it is claimed to measure.** The same paper says the dataset is used to train and evaluate factuality probes. Papers do not link it to any named constructs or behavioral indicators in recorded relationships.

**Space of uses.** The description positions it as a standalone dataset for training and evaluation, rather than alongside any other named benchmark or instrument. No other measurement instruments are linked to it.
