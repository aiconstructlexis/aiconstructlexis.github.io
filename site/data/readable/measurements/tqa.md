# TQA
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 4  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across papers, TQA is most commonly described as Textbook Question Answering. One description presents it as a reading-comprehension benchmark used in generative format, and another as a dataset consisting of scientific textbooks for evaluating knowledge-based reasoning. A smaller line of work uses the same label for TriviaQA, described as a challenging reading-comprehension dataset of question-answer pairs authored by trivia enthusiasts and used for closed-book QA.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers do not link TQA to any recorded construct or behavioral indicator through formal relationships. In authors' own descriptions, it is instead used to assess reading comprehension, and one paper also frames it as evaluating knowledge-based reasoning; a smaller line of work uses the label for closed-book QA.

**Space of uses.** One paper places TQA alongside HumanEval, GSM8k, and NQ in a four-task suite spanning code, math, question answering, and reading comprehension, and reports F1 scores for TQA together with GSM8k and NQ. Elsewhere, one paper treats TQA as a closed-book QA dataset, while another uses the scientific-textbook version of TQA for fine-tuning.
