# IndustryOR
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 5  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the cited papers, IndustryOR is most commonly described as an open-source dataset of commercial Operations Research problems. One paper says it served as the basis for "a novel benchmark dataset based on the open-source commercial IndustryOR" (Decision Information Meets Large Language Models: The Future of Explainable Operations Research), while another describes it as consisting of 100 real-world optimization problems spanning linear, integer, and mixed-integer programming at various difficulty levels.

**What it is claimed to measure.** No papers report IndustryOR as measuring any construct or behavioral indicator.

**Space of uses.** The papers position IndustryOR either as source material for building a new benchmark for explainable OR or as a benchmark used directly for real-world optimization problems at varying difficulty levels. No papers link it to other measurement instruments.
