# TextVQA
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 106  
**Also known as:** TextQA, VQAT, VQAText  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across the surveyed work, the prevailing usage is as a visual question answering benchmark or dataset built around questions that require reading text within images and reasoning over it. Many descriptions add that the images are natural or real-world, text-rich, and tied to OCR or text comprehension, while a smaller line of work frames it more broadly as a document task or as a benchmark for general VLM capabilities. Papers sometimes refer to it as TextQA, VQAT, or VQAText, and some explicitly mention using the validation split.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers use TextVQA to measure 9 constructs and 4 behavioral indicators. The most frequent links treat it as measuring Visual understanding, Fine-grained visual understanding, and General ability, and at the behavioral level Visual question answering and Optical character recognition. Overall, the recorded uses are spread across several capabilities rather than tied to a single construct.

**Space of uses.** Across evaluations, papers usually include TextVQA in broader benchmark suites, commonly alongside VQAv2, GQA, DocVQA, ScienceQA, and VizWiz. It is often presented as one component of general multimodal or VQA evaluation, but some papers single it out for text-oriented, OCR-related, document, or detail-sensitive checks, and a few explicitly use it in few-shot or in-context settings.

**Concerns raised.** A smaller set of papers describes TextVQA as an OCR-extensive or detail-sensitive benchmark that is "highly sensitive to potential information loss in vision tokens." Other evaluation setups note results without providing OCR tokens, and one paper reports a nearly 40% positive gap between random guessing and vision-disabled scores on TextVQA.
