# C4
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 110  
**Also known as:** c4 en-validation, C4 validation loss, C4 validation set, C4 Corpus  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across these papers, the prevailing description of C4 is the Colossal Clean Crawled/Common Crawl corpus, a large cleaned web-text dataset derived from Common Crawl. Some authors refer specifically to the English subset, the validation split or c4 en-validation, including C4 validation loss, while a smaller line uses C4 Corpus for neuron-activation analysis inputs. Beyond evaluation, papers also use it for pre-training or continual language modeling, as calibration data for quantization or pruning, and as a prompt source for text generation.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link C4 to 2 constructs and 1 behavioral indicator. The prevailing use is to measure language modeling ability, typically through perplexity or validation loss on C4 or its validation split; a smaller line of work uses it to measure generative ability, and papers also use it to measure the behavioral indicator text generation. Taken together, the linked claims center on language modeling and generation.

**Space of uses.** Most papers position C4 as one of several evaluation corpora, commonly alongside WikiText2 and often PTB when reporting perplexity. Several papers also use it as calibration data, and one paper says it is "routinely used as a source of calibration data" (Self-calibration for Language Model Quantization and Pruning); other work uses C4 training data for pre-training or continual language modeling, or uses C4 text as prompts for open-ended generation. No other measurement instrument is linked to it.
