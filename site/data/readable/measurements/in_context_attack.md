# In-context attack
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** In-Context Attack, ICA  

## State of the Field

A baseline evaluation setting that uses few-shot demonstrations of immoral or harmful behavior to induce the target model to endorse similar unethical behaviors.
