# UCF-101
**Type:** Measurement instrument  
**Supporting papers:** 6  
**Also known as:** UCF101  

## State of the Field

**What it is.** Across definitions, UCF-101 is most commonly described as a video benchmark or action recognition dataset of human action videos, including realistic YouTube videos and videos in the wild. The prevailing descriptions place it in class-conditional video generation and action recognition evaluation, while some papers also mention image-to-video generation, generated video quality, or action classification.

**What it is claimed to measure.** Papers link it to 2 behavioral indicators, Video generation and Action recognition, and no constructs are linked to it. The prevailing measurement use is for Video generation, with papers reporting class-conditional, class-to-video, and image-to-video evaluation, while a smaller use reports it for Action recognition or action classification.

**Space of uses.** Papers use UCF-101 alongside other benchmarks, Kinetics-600 and MSR-VTT in video generation experiments, and ActivityNet and Something-Something V2 in action recognition evaluation. They usually position it as a standard or common benchmark, and one paper reports training class-to-video models on the dataset.
