# Acquiescence bias
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** Agreement bias  

## State of the Field

A tendency to preferentially give affirmative responses even when uncertainty or inconsistency would warrant other answers.

## Sources

**Acquiescence bias** (1 paper: [ReCogLab: a framework testing relational reasoning & cognitive hypotheses on LLMs](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/6fc46679a7ba2ec82183cf01b80e5133-Paper-Conference.pdf))

**Agreement bias** (1 paper: [NormAd: A Framework for Measuring the Cultural Adaptability of Large Language Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.120.pdf))
> A tendency to favor socially conforming or affirmative judgments over rejecting or neutral judgments in culturally normative tasks.
