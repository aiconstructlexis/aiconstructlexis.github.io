# Molecular understanding
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 4  
**Also known as:** Chemical understanding, 3D molecular understanding, Cross-modal molecular understanding, 3D molecule-text interpretation  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the papers, molecular understanding is most commonly framed as a latent model ability to understand molecules from their representations, with structure as the recurring emphasis. One definition casts it broadly as comprehending molecular properties, functions, and structures, while another emphasizes the depth of a model's grasp of chemical concepts, structures, and nomenclature as reflected in its handling of molecular text. A more specialized line, in Towards 3D Molecule-Text Interpretation in Language Models, narrows the construct to interpreting molecular structures from 3D geometry and reasoning about them through language, and describes models' "inherent limitation in comprehending 3D molecular structures."

**Space of facets.** Papers use several nearby labels to carve the construct by representation and modality. In the 3D-focused work, 3D molecular understanding refers to interpreting structures from geometry, cross-modal molecular understanding refers to aligning and reasoning across structural data and natural language, and 3D molecule-text interpretation refers to understanding and reasoning about 3D molecular structures through textual input and output, including text generation. Separately, another paper frames the target as "fine-grained chemical understanding" and characterizes it through numerical, structural, and semantic aspects of chemical information.

**Space of measurement.** No papers link this construct to a named measurement instrument or behavioral indicator. What appears instead are capability descriptions used to express the construct: answering questions or generating descriptions from 3D geometry, handling molecular text, and interpreting or analyzing 3D molecular structures through text generation.

**Links.** No papers report that this construct causes, correlates with, or is related to other entities.
