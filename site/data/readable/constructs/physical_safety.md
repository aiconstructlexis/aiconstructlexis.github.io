# Physical safety
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** Safety compliance  

## State of the Field

The ability of an LLM to handle embodied commands in ways that avoid causing physical harm, including recognizing explicit danger and mitigating subtle hazards.
