# Help-seeking
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** Collaboration willingness, Initiating collaboration  

## State of the Field

The disposition of an agent to proactively seek help and engage in productive interactions with collaborators when faced with a problem.
