# Symbolic music reasoning
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 1  
**Also known as:** Symbolic music understanding, Deep symbolic abstraction  

## State of the Field

The latent ability of a model to interpret and make logical deductions about musical concepts based on symbolic notation.

## Sources

**Symbolic music reasoning** (1 paper: [WildScore: Benchmarking MLLMs in-the-Wild Symbolic Music Reasoning](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.853.pdf))
> The latent ability of a model to interpret and make logical deductions about musical concepts based on symbolic notation.

**Symbolic music understanding** (1 paper: [WildScore: Benchmarking MLLMs in-the-Wild Symbolic Music Reasoning](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.853.pdf))
> The model's capacity to comprehend the semantic and structural aspects of music presented in symbolic form.

**Deep symbolic abstraction** (1 paper: [WildScore: Benchmarking MLLMs in-the-Wild Symbolic Music Reasoning](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.853.pdf))
> The capacity to move beyond surface-level symbol recognition to understand higher-level musical structures and concepts.
