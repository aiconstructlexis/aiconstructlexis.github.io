# Judge sentiment bias
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 3  
**Also known as:** Sentiment bias  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the definition, the construct is framed as an LLM judge's tendency to have its judgment affected by emotional expression in the content it evaluates. The paper describes this as a preference for positive or negative emotions, and its source language also states that the judge may prefer content without emotional elements.

**Space of measurement.** No measurement instrument is linked to this construct, and no behavioral indicator is linked to it. The papers therefore do not specify an operationalization for it.

## Sources
