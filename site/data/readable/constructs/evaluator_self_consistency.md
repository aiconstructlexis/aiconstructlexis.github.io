# Evaluator self-consistency
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 4  
**Also known as:** Self-alignment  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** The paper treats this construct, under the name "Self-alignment," as the extent to which an evaluator's internal reasoning and scoring are consistent with its own task understanding and instructions. Its usage is tied to "internal thinking": the paper says it instructs GPT to conduct internal thinking that aligns itself for better task and preference understanding, and presents this internal thinking as strengthening task understanding and instruction following.

**Space of measurement.** Papers do not link this construct to any measurement instrument or behavioral indicator. The material therefore does not specify a benchmark, human evaluation setup, judge-model procedure, probe, or analysis that operationalizes it.

**Links.** No causal, correlational, or related links are reported for this construct.
