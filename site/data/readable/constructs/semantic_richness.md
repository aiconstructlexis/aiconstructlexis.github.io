# Semantic richness
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 3  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** In one paper, the construct is framed in a caption-centered way: semantic richness is the degree to which generated captions capture complex, diverse, and contextually grounded meanings, rather than simple or template-like descriptions. The same paper also characterizes semantically richer captions as having richer vocabulary, being longer and more diverse, and describing content in a more complex manner.

**Space of facets.** The use does not introduce named subtypes, broader construct hierarchies, or competing labels. Instead, the construct is presented alongside length, vocabulary diversity, readability complexity, and complexity of description when characterizing caption outputs.

**Space of measurement.** No measurement instrument or behavioral indicator is linked to it. Operationally, the only cues provided are output-level properties of captions, namely greater length, richer and more diverse vocabulary, higher readability complexity, and more complex descriptions, rather than a named benchmark, human evaluation protocol, LLM-as-a-judge setup, or probe.
