# AI R&D ability
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** AI R&D capability, ML R&D capability, Research engineering skill  

## State of the Field

The latent ability of an AI agent to autonomously perform the complex, open-ended tasks involved in advancing artificial intelligence research and development.

## Sources

**AI R&D capability** (1 paper: [RE-Bench: Evaluating Frontier AI R&D Capabilities of Language Model Agents against Human Experts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v267/main/assets/wijk25a/wijk25a.pdf))
> The latent ability of an AI agent to autonomously perform the complex, open-ended tasks involved in advancing artificial intelligence research and development.

**ML R&D capability** (1 paper: [PaperBench: Evaluating AI’s Ability to Replicate AI Research](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v267/main/assets/starace25a/starace25a.pdf))
> The latent ability of an AI agent to perform tasks associated with machine learning research and development, such as understanding, implementing, and experimentally validating novel concepts from academic papers.

**Research engineering skill** (1 paper: [RE-Bench: Evaluating Frontier AI R&D Capabilities of Language Model Agents against Human Experts](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mlresearch/v267/main/assets/wijk25a/wijk25a.pdf))
> The underlying competence to make progress on open-ended machine learning research engineering problems through experimentation, implementation, and compute-efficient problem solving.
