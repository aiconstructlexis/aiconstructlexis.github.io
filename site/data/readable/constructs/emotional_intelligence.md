# Emotional intelligence
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 6  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the papers, the prevailing usage treats emotional intelligence as a model, agent, or system ability, sometimes explicitly a latent ability, to perceive or understand human emotions and respond appropriately, usually in conversation. Some authors broaden this to managing emotions in social interactions or interpreting social situations, especially interpersonal conflict, and one definition extends the target to the emotions of oneself and others. The main variation is scope, from conversational emotion handling to broader social interaction and conflict-resolution settings.

**Space of facets.** One explicit decomposition treats emotional fidelity as an aspect of emotional intelligence, with emotional fidelity framed as part of a broader capability in social interaction. Elsewhere, papers organize the construct by setting rather than named subtypes, especially emotional support dialogue and interpersonal conflict resolution. One study also places emotional intelligence alongside other highly subjective tasks such as creative writing and persuasiveness.

**Space of measurement.** Papers link emotional intelligence to one measurement instrument: it is measured by EQ-Bench, described as a benchmark, and no behavioral indicators are linked to it. Beyond that explicit benchmark link, one study uses an LLM-as-a-judge setup, deploying a council of recent LLMs to rank each other on open-ended responses to interpersonal conflicts. That operationalization follows the framing of emotional intelligence around understanding and responding appropriately to emotions and social situations in conflict.

**Links.** Beyond measurement, papers link emotional intelligence to one construct, creativity. One paper claims a causal relationship from emotional intelligence to creativity, stating that reasoning tasks needing emotional intelligence "influence the relative creation tasks." No correlational or generic related links are reported.
