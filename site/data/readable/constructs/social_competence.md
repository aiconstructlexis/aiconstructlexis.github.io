# Social competence
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 3  
**Also known as:** Social awareness, Social understanding  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the papers, the shared framing is a latent social ability or quality, but authors place it on different targets. One usage treats it as the quality of conversational agent responses to behave appropriately in social interaction and avoid socially deficient replies, with socially deficient responses described as a kind of error. Other papers frame nearby notions as the ability to handle socially grounded judgments or commonsense social situations in language tasks, or to recognize and interpret subtle, implicit social cues such as intentions and implications beyond literal text.

**Space of facets.** The papers use adjacent labels, social competence, social awareness, and social understanding, rather than a single settled terminology. The clearest difference is scope and level: the construct is framed either as a property of responses in interaction, a capability for socially grounded judgment tasks, or an interpretive ability centered on implicit cues and meanings beyond literal text.

**Space of measurement.** Papers do not link this construct to any measurement instrument or behavioral indicator, so no operationalization is specified here.

**Links.** Papers do not report this construct as causing, correlating with, or being related to other entities.
