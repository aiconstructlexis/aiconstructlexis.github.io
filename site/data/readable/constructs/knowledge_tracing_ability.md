# Knowledge tracing ability
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 3  
**Also known as:** Predictive accuracy  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** The only definition treats knowledge tracing ability as a latent predictive capacity: correctly forecasting a student's future performance on educational tasks from historical interaction data and evolving knowledge states. The accompanying descriptions keep that framing tightly centered on prediction, using phrases such as "accurately predict future performance," "forecast student performance," and improved "prediction accuracy" and "overall predictive performance."

**Space of measurement.** No measurement instrument or behavioral indicator is linked to it. Instead, the paper describes it through predictive outcomes, especially prediction accuracy, forecasting future performance, and overall predictive performance.
