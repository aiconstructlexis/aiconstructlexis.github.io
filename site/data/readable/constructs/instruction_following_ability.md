## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the papers, the prevailing usage treats this as a latent capacity or tendency to understand natural-language instructions and produce responses or actions that satisfy them. Most definitions emphasize adherence to explicit constraints, requested formats, and user intent, and, as "Evaluating Large Language Models at Evaluating Instruction Following" puts it, it is "the ability to correctly parse open-ended instructions and adhere to the specified requirements." A smaller line of work treats it more behaviorally, as the observable act of generating outputs that stay on task, follow formats, or carry out action sequences, and some papers narrow it to multimodal, multilingual, retrieval, tool-use, or rule-bound settings.

**Space of facets.** Papers do not settle on one subtyping scheme, but they repeatedly narrow the construct by what is being followed, for example domain guidelines, prompt or system-message constraints, and formal rules or principles. Another recurring split is scope: general versus open-ended instruction following, single-turn compliance versus maintaining constraints across turns, and text-only versus multimodal, multilingual, spoken, retrieval, or embodied uses. Papers also use near-synonyms such as task adherence, instruction fidelity, and instruction compliance, and one evaluation framing divides instruction following into relevance, factuality, and adherence.

**Space of measurement.** Operationalization is broad: papers link the construct to 22 measurement instruments and 6 behavioral indicators. The most frequently used instruments are MT-Bench, AlpacaEval 2.0, IFEval, Arena-Hard, and AlpacaEval, mostly benchmarks, while some work also uses human studies or model-judge setups; the measured behaviors include staying within word limits, producing valid JSON, answering in the requested format, and maintaining system-message constraints over multiple turns. These operationalizations usually match the dominant definitions of adhering to instructions and constraints, although some are broader, for example using overall generation quality or judge-based comparison as the measure, as in "NEFTune: Noisy Embeddings Improve Instruction Finetuning," which adopts AlpacaEval as a central measure.

**Links.** Links beyond measurement are dispersed and usually reported by one paper each. Several papers claim causal relationships from instruction-following ability to tool use and to several other downstream properties, while separate papers claim causal relationships from self-correction and stability to instruction-following ability; one paper also reports that instruction-following ability correlates negatively with reasoning in reasoning tasks. Other papers describe it as related to alignment and to many additional neighboring constructs and behaviors.
