# Speech suitability
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 1  
**Also known as:** Speech-suitability  

## State of the Field

The degree to which a model's output is appropriate for spoken delivery, encompassing naturalness, conciseness, and ease of comprehension when heard rather than read.
