# Morphosyntactic competence
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** Morpho-syntactic competence  

## State of the Field

The model's integrated ability to process and understand both morphological (word structure) and syntactic (sentence structure) information in context.

## Sources

**Morphosyntactic competence** (1 paper: [Extracting Linguistic Information from Large Language Models: Syntactic Relations and Derivational Knowledge](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1384.pdf))
> The model's integrated ability to process and understand both morphological (word structure) and syntactic (sentence structure) information in context.

**Morpho-syntactic competence** (1 paper: [Exploring morphology-aware tokenization: A case study on Spanish language modeling](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1552.pdf))
> The latent ability of a model to capture syntactic dependencies governed by morphological features such as gender, number, and person agreement in a fusional language.
