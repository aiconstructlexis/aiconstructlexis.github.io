# Inter-agent trust
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** Trust  

## State of the Field

A latent positive disposition developed by an agent towards its peers based on their historical accuracy, which can increase its propensity to conform to their subsequent judgments.
