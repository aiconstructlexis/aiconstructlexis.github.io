# Coarse perception
**Type:** Construct  
**Supporting papers:** 3  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** In the usage in the papers, coarse perception is treated as a latent ability: perceiving and describing high-level, general elements within an image. The only explicit definition comes from Prism: A Framework for Decoupling and Assessing the Capabilities of VLMs, which also describes models as showing "strong coarse and fine-grained perceptual abilities" and being "equally adept at handling coarse perception contents."

**Space of facets.** The concept is only lightly systematized in the cited material. The paper explicitly places it alongside "fine-grained perceptual abilities" and describes coarse perception in terms of "high-level, general elements within an image."

**Space of measurement.** No measurement instrument or behavioral indicator is linked to coarse perception, and the cited material does not name a benchmark, human evaluation protocol, judge model, or probe for operationalizing it.
