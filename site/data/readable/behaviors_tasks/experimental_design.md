# Experimental design
**Type:** Behavioral indicator  
**Supporting papers:** 4  
**Also known as:** Experiment design  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Across the surveyed work, the prevailing usage is a task of outlining experiments for a research paper or research idea, usually to validate a proposed method, problem, solution, or idea. Definitions converge on producing the setup, procedures, or plan for experiments, and some formulations make the expected output more specific, such as a brief experiment plan with explanations or a list of experiments with their motivations; one definition also explicitly allows this within papers whose results are simulated.

**Variants.** Papers use both "Experimental design" and "Experiment design" for closely matching formulations. The behavior appears in settings where the model is given pre-experiment or partial-paper context, and in broader research systems that also define problems and propose methods; the requested output is variously framed as an experiment plan with explanations, a list of experiments with motivations, or an outline of procedures.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to this behavior in the papers.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** Papers do not link this behavior to any construct.
