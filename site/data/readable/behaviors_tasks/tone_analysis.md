# Tone analysis
**Type:** Behavioral indicator  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** Tone classification, Tone profiling, Tone intensity scoring  

## State of the Field

Identifying the rhetorical tone of a text, such as sarcasm or satire, to assess its potential for misleading interpretation.

## Sources

**Tone analysis** (1 paper: [TRUST-VL: An Explainable News Assistant for General Multimodal Misinformation Detection](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.284.pdf))
> Identifying the rhetorical tone of a text, such as sarcasm or satire, to assess its potential for misleading interpretation.

**Tone classification** (1 paper: [Comprehensive and Efficient Distillation for Lightweight Sentiment Analysis Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1122.pdf))
> Classifying a text into tone categories such as positive, negative, neutral, sarcastic, or ironic.

**Tone intensity scoring** (1 paper: [Comprehensive and Efficient Distillation for Lightweight Sentiment Analysis Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1122.pdf))
> The task of assigning a numerical score to represent the intensity of the tone in a given text.

**Tone profiling** (1 paper: [Comprehensive and Efficient Distillation for Lightweight Sentiment Analysis Models](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.1122.pdf))
> Producing a structured representation of a text's tone, including category, intensity, and emotional undertones.
