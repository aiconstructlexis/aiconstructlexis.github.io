**Type:** Behavioral indicator  
**Supporting papers:** 3

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Definitions treat color recognition as identifying and naming the colors of objects in an image. One paper also places it under a "color" question category, so the task is framed as image-based color judgments about objects.

**Variants.** Terminology is limited. One paper uses "Color identification," while the linked instrument names a related question type, "Color Diversity," described as testing the ability to distinguish and identify items based on color.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** One measurement instrument is linked to it, Waste-Bench. In that instrument, "Color Diversity" is the recorded question type used to measure this behavior, and it is described as testing whether a model can distinguish and identify items based on color.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No papers treat color recognition as evidence of a construct.
