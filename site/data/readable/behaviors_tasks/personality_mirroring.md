# Personality mirroring
**Type:** Behavioral indicator  
**Supporting papers:** 2  
**Also known as:** Conversational adaptation, Linguistic coordination, Tone imitation  

## State of the Field

The observable alignment of language use between conversational partners at the level of the entire dialogue.

## Sources

**Linguistic coordination** (1 paper: [Evaluating Behavioral Alignment in Conflict Dialogue: A Multi-Dimensional Comparison of LLM Agents and Humans](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.828.pdf))
> The observable alignment of language use between conversational partners at the level of the entire dialogue.

**Personality mirroring** (1 paper: [Chameleon LLMs: User Personas Influence Chatbot Personality Shifts](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.875.pdf))
> The observable act of a chatbot adopting the linguistic style, tone, and behavioral tendencies of the user it is interacting with.

**Conversational adaptation** (1 paper: [Chameleon LLMs: User Personas Influence Chatbot Personality Shifts](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.875.pdf))
> Observable shift in the tone, style, or content of an LLM's responses during a dialogue that aligns with the perceived personality of the user.

**Tone imitation** (1 paper: [Chameleon LLMs: User Personas Influence Chatbot Personality Shifts](https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.875.pdf))
> Observable use of linguistic style, punctuation, and emotional valence in responses that reflect the user's tone, such as enthusiasm or cynicism.
