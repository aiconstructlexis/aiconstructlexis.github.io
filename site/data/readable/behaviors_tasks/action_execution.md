## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** The only explicit framing treats action execution as the agent's observable performance of a specific action from its available action space within an environment. **Variants.** Papers do not distinguish named subtypes, domains, or modalities in the descriptions. The framing stays at the level of one category of agentic task, defined relative to the task, the environment, and the agent's available action space.
