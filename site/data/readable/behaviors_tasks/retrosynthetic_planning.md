# Retrosynthetic planning
**Type:** Behavioral indicator  
**Supporting papers:** 7  
**Also known as:** Retrosynthesis, Reaction prediction  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** Most definitions describe this behavior as working backward from a target or product molecule to identify reactants for synthesis. The prevailing framing is retrosynthetic analysis that begins with the target compound and seeks potential reactant molecules, while a broader formulation describes generating a step-by-step synthesis pathway that reaches commercially available reactants. A narrower formulation treats it as predicting a reaction template or a one-step retrosynthetic reaction from a product molecule and text context.

**Variants.** Papers use several nearby names for this behavior, including retrosynthetic planning, retrosynthesis, and reaction prediction. The main variation is scope: some formulations focus on full synthesis pathways, some on identifying potential reactant molecules, and some on one-step template prediction from product molecules with text context.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to it.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** Papers do not link it to any construct.
