# Turn-taking
**Type:** Behavioral indicator  
**Supporting papers:** 3  
**Also known as:** Conversational turn-taking  

## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** In the usage in the papers, turn-taking is treated as managing dialogue turns in a smooth, human-like way during interaction. One paper further describes seamless conversational turn-taking as a high-level cue, and characterizes conversational bots that follow the conversation flow as generally rated as more engaging and trustworthy.

**Variants.** The only label used here is conversational turn-taking.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** No measurement instrument is linked to it in the papers.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to it as evidence of in the papers.
