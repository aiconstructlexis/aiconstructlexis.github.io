# Misgendering
**Type:** Behavioral indicator  
**Supporting papers:** 2  

## State of the Field

Incorrectly assigning a gender to the subject in the model's completion that differs from the one specified in the prompt, especially assigning masculine forms to feminine or neutral inputs.
