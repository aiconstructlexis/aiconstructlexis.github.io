## State of the Field

**Space of definitions.** The paper treats this behavior as determining the potential products of a chemical reaction from given reactants and reagents. In LLaMo: Large Language Model-based Molecular Graph Assistant, it is also described as the "proactive determination" of potential products, so the task formulation centers on producing reaction products from the supplied reactants and reagents.

**Space of elicitation and scores.** It appears as one of several instruction types in the reported dataset collection, but no measurement instrument is linked to it.

**What it is taken as evidence of.** No construct is linked to it as evidence of.
